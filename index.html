How grading works

Send any Holo or Ultra Rare to PSA. Grades go from PSA 1 (Poor) to PSA 10 (Gem Mint). Chance for a 10 is 15 %. Lower grades show whitening, scratches, creases and stains. Every grade is final. A slab can never be cracked or resubmitted. A PSA 10 holo also unlocks the next set.
