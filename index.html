PSA VAULT

How grading works

Send any Holo or Ultra Rare from your binder to PSA. Scale from PSA 1 (Poor) to PSA 10 (Gem Mint). Chance for a 10 is 15 %, close to real vintage gem rates. Lower grades show visible whitening, scratches, creases and stains. Crack a slab to resubmit and gamble on a better grade.
